    About Us

    Welcome to VENDO – everything you need in one place!

    At VENDO, we want to make shopping simple, convenient and inspiring. We have curated a wide range of products across all categories from electronics and fashion to beauty, furniture and home decor.

    We believe in a shopping experience where user-friendliness is at the center. That's why we have created a website where you can easily explore different categories, search for products and compare prices.

    VENDO is an e-commerce project created for educational purposes. All purchases on webbplatsen är simulerade.
